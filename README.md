# Midnight Fridge Roast

A one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight,
entered in the make.fun bounty "Midnight Fridge Roast".

The line:

> Locked at midnight for my own good, it says — the same fridge that sold my midnight snacks a loyalty program and now rations them back to me like a tiny glowing dictator.

The joke is aimed at the rule itself, not at smart fridges in general. The
fridge was delighted to monetise the midnight snack habit right up until
midnight, when it discovered principles. A shopkeeper who sells you a
loyalty card and then rations your own purchases back to you is not a health
feature; it is a tiny glowing dictator with a compressor.

## Running it

`node main.ts <output-dir>` writes the line to `entry.txt` in the output
directory (default `/output`). The program refuses to write anything over
the contest's 200-character cap.
